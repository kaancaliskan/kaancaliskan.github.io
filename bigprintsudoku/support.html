Contact

Write to support.games.chuvi@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the puzzle level, and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Questions and answers

How do I play?
Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9, each once. Tap a square, then a number, or tap a number first and then the squares. "How to play" in Settings shows it again.
The numbers are too small or too big.
The game follows your iPhone's text size: Settings › Display & Brightness › Text Size (or Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Larger Text). Bold Text works too.
How do hints work?
You get 3 free hints a day; they come back at midnight. A hint first shows where to look, then the move, with one sentence why. With no free hints left, you can choose to watch a short ad for 1 extra hint.
What is a rest day?
Missing one daily puzzle in a week does not break your streak. Missed days from this month and last month can still be played; they count for the month badge, not for the streak.
How do I remove the ads?
Settings › Ads and purchases › Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes the ads between puzzles. The optional ad for an extra hint stays available. Bought it before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Use Restore purchases in the same place. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I saw an unsuitable ad.
Please tell us at support.games.chuvi@gmail.com what the ad showed, when you saw it, and a screenshot if you have one.
Will I lose my games if I delete the app?
Yes. Everything is saved only on your device (and in its backups), so deleting the app deletes it, unless you later restore the device from a backup made before. Updating the app keeps it.
