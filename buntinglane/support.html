Bunting Lane Support

Calm shelf-sorting puzzles for iPhone

Contact

Write to support.games.chuvi@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version and the level number, and what happened. We usually answer within a few days.

Questions and answers

How do I play?
Drag a good onto a shelf with a free spot. Three of the same on one shelf are packed away. Goods behind the front row step forward when a shelf is cleared.
The clock feels too fast.
Turn on Relaxed mode in Settings: no clock, play at your own pace. After a few tries on a hard level the game also offers "Make this calmer".
I ran out of lives.
A new life comes back every 30 minutes. The time of the next one is shown on the home screen.
Can I get a life back sooner?
With no lives left, you can choose to watch one short ad for 1 life, on the home screen or after a lost level. It is always your choice; the next free life still comes on time.
How do I remove the ads?
Settings › Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available. Bought it before, or on another iPhone with the same Apple Account? Use Settings › Restore purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I saw an unsuitable ad.
Please tell us at support.games.chuvi@gmail.com: what the ad showed, when and where you saw it, and a screenshot if you have one (Settings › About this game › Report an ad).
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Your progress is saved only on your iPhone (and in its backups), so deleting the app deletes it, unless you later restore the iPhone from a backup made before. Updating the app keeps it.
Some goods look alike to me.
Tell us which ones. Every good is checked for look-alikes, also for colour blindness, but your feedback helps.
Why do some goods only appear at certain times of year?
Halloween, Christmas, Easter and summer goods visit the shop in their own season.
