Bunting Lane Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Bunting Lane app for iPhone

Bunting Lane is a sorting puzzle game made by an independent developer ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses, why, how long it is kept and what your rights are. In short: Bunting Lane has no accounts, does not track you, and keeps your progress on your iPhone.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is the developer of Bunting Lane. E-mail: support.games.chuvi@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (levels, stars, gold, boosters, lives), your settings (sound, music, vibration, Relaxed mode and the other comfort options) and the levels the game builds for you are saved only on your iPhone, and in your iPhone's backups (iCloud or a computer) if you use them. We cannot see any of it. It stays until you choose Settings › Reset all progress or delete the app.

What we collect

The game itself sends us nothing. It has no sign-in, no analytics and no contact lists, and it does not ask for your location, photos, camera or microphone.

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and what you write. We use it only to answer you (our legitimate interest in helping you), we never share it, and we delete it within 12 months of our last reply.

Advertising

Bunting Lane is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a short ad after some won levels (at most every second win, from Level 6, and none on your first day) and optional ads you can choose to watch for a reward. Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message where the law requires it. Where you agree, that consent is the legal basis. If you decline, Google may still show limited ads that use no cookies or identifiers for personalisation (to keep the game free).

The ads are never personalised: they are not based on what you do in other apps or on websites. The game never asks for permission to track you, so iOS keeps your advertising identifier from the ads (they see only zeros).

Even so, to show ads, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and count what was shown, Google receives technical information: your device model and operating system, an app-instance or device identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, which ads were shown and tapped, and technical crash and performance details of its ad software. We do not receive this information and cannot use it to identify you.

Google decides for itself how it uses and keeps this information, as an independent controller. Google protects it at least as well as this policy does and may process it in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, how long Google keeps data and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the game's Settings › Privacy options (shown wherever Google's consent message applies). Withdrawing it does not affect ads already shown. The game also saves on your iPhone how many ads it has shown and when, so it can space them out; this never leaves your iPhone.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is sold and processed by Apple. We never see your name or payment details; Apple only tells the game that the purchase was made. You can restore it in Settings › Restore purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How long information is kept

We keep no information about your play. What is on your iPhone stays until you reset or delete the game. Support e-mails are deleted within 12 months of our last reply.

Children

Bunting Lane is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, for example by e-mail, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. In practice we hold only the e-mails you send us; write to us and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority where you live.

Changes

If the game changes what it uses, we will update this page and the date at the top.
