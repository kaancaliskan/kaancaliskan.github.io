Pitchmind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 (advertising added) · Applies to the Pitchmind app for iPhone

Pitchmind is a football knowledge game made by an independent developer, Chuvi Games ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses. In short: Pitchmind has no accounts, does not track you, shows only ads that are not personalised, and keeps your progress on your device.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is the developer of Pitchmind. E-mail: support.games.chuvi@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your points, unlocked sessions, statistics, achievements, hints, your squad in My Eleven, your duel rating and results, the nickname you choose and your settings are saved only on your iPhone, and in your device's backups (iCloud or a computer) if you use them. We cannot see any of it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Derby Duel with a friend nearby

Derby Duel connects two iPhones that are close to each other over your local Wi-Fi or a direct peer-to-peer link (Apple's MultipeerConnectivity). iOS asks for permission to use the local network the first time you open it. During a duel the two phones exchange only what the match needs: your nickname, your player card (rating and division), your answers and timings, and the reactions you tap. This goes directly from phone to phone, encrypted, and never through us or any server. Nothing is kept by us.

What we collect

The game itself sends us nothing. It has no sign-in, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and it does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. Solo play works without any internet connection.

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and what you write. We use it only to answer you (our legitimate interest in helping you), we never share it, and we delete it within 12 months of our last reply.

Advertising

Pitchmind is free and shows ads from Google AdMob in two places only: a short ad after you close the results of a finished session (never during a question, never in practice or in duels, none in your first three sessions, at most after every second session and never twice within four minutes), and optional ads you can choose to watch for two hints or for a star player in My Eleven. Before any ad is requested, the game shows Google's consent message where the law requires it. Where you agree, that consent is the legal basis. If you decline, Google may still show limited ads that use no cookies or identifiers for personalisation.

The ads are never personalised: they are not based on what you do in other apps or on websites. The game never asks for permission to track you, so iOS keeps your advertising identifier from the ads.

To show ads, limit how often you see the same one, prevent fraud and count what was shown, Google receives technical information: your device model and operating system, an app-instance or device identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, which ads were shown and tapped, and technical crash and performance details of its ad software. We do not receive this information and cannot use it to identify you.

Google decides for itself how it uses and keeps this information, as an independent controller, and may process it in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where Google's consent message applies, you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Profile › Settings › Privacy options. The game also saves on your device how many sessions you have finished since the last ad and when it was shown, so it can space ads out; this never leaves your device.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase sold and processed by Apple. It removes the ads between sessions; the optional ads you choose to watch stay available. We never see your name or payment details; Apple only tells the game that the purchase was made. You can restore it in Profile › Shop › Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Children

Pitchmind is a general-audience game made with adults in mind and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. In practice we hold only the e-mails you send us; write to us and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority where you live.

Changes

If the game changes what it uses, we will update this page and the date at the top.
