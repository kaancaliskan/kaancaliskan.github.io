Pitchmind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026 · Applies to the Pitchmind app for iPhone

Pitchmind is a football knowledge game made by an independent developer, Chuvi Games ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses. In short: Pitchmind has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it keeps your progress on your device.

Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is the developer of Pitchmind. E-mail: support.games.chuvi@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your points, unlocked sessions, statistics, achievements, hints, your squad in My Eleven, your duel rating and results, the nickname you choose and your settings are saved only on your iPhone, and in your device's backups (iCloud or a computer) if you use them. We cannot see any of it. Settings › Reset all progress erases it, and deleting the app deletes it.

Derby Duel with a friend nearby

Derby Duel connects two iPhones that are close to each other over your local Wi-Fi or a direct peer-to-peer link (Apple's MultipeerConnectivity). iOS asks for permission to use the local network the first time you open it. During a duel the two phones exchange only what the match needs: your nickname, your player card (rating and division), your answers and timings, and the reactions you tap. This goes directly from phone to phone, encrypted, and never through us or any server. Nothing is kept by us.

What we collect

The game itself sends us nothing. It has no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting of its own, and it does not ask for your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone. Solo play works without any internet connection.

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and what you write. We use it only to answer you (our legitimate interest in helping you), we never share it, and we delete it within 12 months of our last reply.

Advertising and purchases

This version of Pitchmind shows no ads and offers no in-app purchases. If that changes, we will update this page and the date at the top before the update is released.

Children

Pitchmind is a general-audience game made with adults in mind and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. In practice we hold only the e-mails you send us; write to us and we will answer within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority, in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority where you live.

Changes

If the game changes what it uses, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
