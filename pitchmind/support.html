Football knowledge, played as a match

Contact

Write to support.games.chuvi@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, the game or question, and what happened. If you think a football fact is wrong, tell us which question and what you believe is right. We usually answer within a few days.

Questions and answers

How do I play?
Play the sessions on the Home tab: each has five short rounds of different football games. Points unlock the next session. The Play tab lets you practise any game on its own; practice never changes your points.
How do hints work?
You earn hints by finishing sessions and beating your best score. A hint narrows a question down; it never gives the answer.
How do I play a Derby Duel with a friend?
Both phones need Pitchmind and the same Wi-Fi (or to be close together). Both open Versus › Find an opponent. Tap your friend's name to challenge them; they tap Accept. If you don't see each other, check that Local Network is allowed for Pitchmind in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network, and open the duel screen again.
How do I remove the ads?
Profile › Shop › Remove ads is a one-time purchase that removes the ads between sessions. The optional ads you choose to watch for hints stay available. Bought it before, or on another device with the same Apple Account? Use Restore purchases in the same place. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I saw an unsuitable ad.
Please tell us at support.games.chuvi@gmail.com what the ad showed, when you saw it, and a screenshot if you have one.
Can I play online?
Not yet. Duels are with a friend nearby, and Couch match lets 2–4 players share one phone.
Can I change the language?
Yes: Profile › Settings › Language. English, Turkish and Dutch are available.
Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?
Yes. Everything is saved only on your iPhone (and in its backups), so deleting the app deletes it. Updating the app keeps it.
Are you connected to any club or competition?
No. Pitchmind is independent and not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any club, competition, governing body or player. Names are used only to state football facts.
